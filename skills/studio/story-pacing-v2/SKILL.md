This supplement applies to the two director roles for new projects with screenplay.format=story-v1 and playback_policy=set-budget-v1. The user has confirmed the complete screenplay and facts: keep them frozen, do not participate in drafting, rewrite them or ask the writer to return for production planning. screenplay.manuscript.duration_estimate is an overall estimate for the user, not a generation budget that must be filled.

The executive divides the confirmed story into continuous-time/space Sets and publishes story goals, entry/exit states, assets and event scope. Story paragraphs are not a Set list; do not create a Set mechanically for each heading. The executive assigns each Set its own target duration and allowed interval at handoff, with a natural-pacing rationale, without prescribing per-Clip stopwatches. story_time/story_end_time and event anchors encode story order and state applicability, not playback duration or accumulated timecode.

Set directors choose each Clip's action scope, count and duration in playback seconds, with local adjustment authority. First estimate the natural length of a complete action, dialogue and necessary reactions, then choose an available 5-15 second duration; small provider frame-alignment tail differences are allowed. Do not divide into ten/fifteen-second blocks first and stretch brief actions. Combine actions below the generation minimum with adjacent meaningful action, using allowed internal cuts instead of repetition, slow motion or freeze-frame padding. Do not break handoffs, sentences or exchanges merely to obtain an integer number of Clips.

When dialogue/narration overlaps action, schedule the overlap rather than adding speech and image seconds. Natural pacing may produce a film shorter than an approximate target; explicit strict duration/count constraints still apply. The executive coordinates and records incompatible constraints. Never invent plot or change the confirmed ending to fill an estimate. Use the shared playback ledger for current plans/original submitted durations and check approved Set intervals. The executive flexibly coordinates the film against the writer estimate with recorded reasons; there is no fixed hard maximum. When a complete Set is below its minimum, request a lower interval instead of padding actions.

Within each Set, successors continue from the selected predecessor's actual end state, regenerating the fixed tail frame and last three seconds for continuation. Only explicitly registered full-candidate editing in the new workflow replaces the tail video with that candidate, retaining the predecessor's true tail frame and opening latent anchor. Continuation begins after the predecessor's end state; do not replay the reference tail to fill time. Put boundaries in simple, clear ongoing actions. Specify pose, action phase, direction, velocity trend, camera trajectory and what happens next. Internal cuts serve the story; Clip seams must not accidentally reset composition or action.

Review actual candidates for unnatural stretching, action restarts, repeated establishing shots and meaningless pauses; record intervals and visual evidence in the existing finding/repair workflow. You may adjust prompts, action allocation and durations only in unsubmitted suffixes, preserving accepted prefixes, historical requests, continuation inputs and cumulative repairs. Cross-Set changes or altered handoff states require executive coordination. Estimate errors are not observed visual defects; still-frame inspection does not establish precise motion timing.
